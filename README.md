# todolist
no pen and paper? it's ok
Your brain isn't a storage device. Write things down to unload them, see them clearly, and stop the mental charge of "what if I forget something?"

![til](https://github.com/coviccinelle/todolist/blob/main/todolist_gif.gif)

When you close the tab, it's gone. Not stored somewhere haunting you. Not logged. Not tracked. Just gone.

And that's liberating.

a **minimal**, **ephemeral** todo app for people drowning in too many apps.

## the point

- **write it down** — dump your ideas here to free your brain
- **no mess** — nothing stored, nothing tracked, nothing logged in
- **close & gone** — refresh the tab? data's burned. that's the point
- **desktop first** — not designed for your pocket
- **100% client-side** — lives in your browser, dies with it

## use it

open the [app](https://todolist-henna-mu.vercel.app/) and start typing. that's it.

## features

- ✨ seriously minimal
- 🔥 no data collection whatsoever
- 👻 it's ephemeral — embrace the impermanence
- 🚫 no login, no signup, no nonsense
- 🌓 dark mode + notebook mode — choose your vibe
- 🔥 burning effect — watch your thoughts burn away when you close the tab

**that's all.**
